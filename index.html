About Me

I am a PhD student with a broad interest in theoretical computer science, specifically in fine-grained algorithm design (and complexity) and applications of randomized rounding and linear programming techniques. Currently, I am supervised by Lars Rohwedder at the University of Southern Denmark (SDU).

I am a strong advocate for making the TCS community a safe and inclusive space for everyone. If you need someone to listen or want to have a respectful conversation about the fact that there is still a long way to go, please reach out or come talk to me.

In my personal life, there is a 99% chance that:

  • You will find on my mat –– doing yoga, functional strength training, HIIT or any combination thereof –– or outside biking or going for a walk; more recently, I also started bouldering.
  • I am listening to music –– I am always counting down the days to the next concert or festival day.
